## About the Band

Formed by guitarist Erik Oldman and drummer/percussionist Paul Abella, the ensemble skips the academic jazz route to focus on groove with a bend for experimentation. The band's core repertoire draws heavily from the driving rhythms of *James Brown*, *Funkadelic*, and *Herbie Hancock*, alongside instrumental arrangements of classic Stax/Volt material from *Booker T. & the M.G.'s* and *Isaac Hayes*. They lean into the gritty soul-jazz of *Eddie Harris* and *Grant Green*, while completely upending expectations with heavy, funky reinterpretations of classic tracks from the likes of *Black Sabbath* and *Fugazi*.

Currently, the band hosts a **Jazz Centennial Monthly Residency**, dedicating full-night performances to the 1926 birth year celebrations of jazz icons **Miles Davis**, **John Coltrane**, **Brother Jack McDuff**, and **Lou Donaldson**—filtering their legendary catalogs through the band's heavy, groove-first lens.

### The Core Lineup

- **Erik Oldman (Guitar & Synth):** A veteran of the Chicago instrumental scene, Erik is a founding member of *Sons of Ra*, the dark ambient drone duo *Mare Crisium*, and free-jazz-fusion ensemble *Late Model*. He shapes the band's sonic atmosphere with heavy rhythmic phrasing, bass-heavy textures, and vintage synth layers.
- **Paul Abella (Drums and Percussion):** Paul brings a sharp, acoustic drive to the core rhythm section, drawing from his work leading the *Paul Abella Trio* and the punk-free-jazz ensemble, *Questionable Decisions*. Paul serves as the Music Director of WDCB 90.9FM Chicago's Home for Jazz.
- **Randy Bufanda (Saxophones):** A versatile woodwind player and essential session saxophonist, Randy performs regularly across the city with *Lost Pyramids* and *Oh God, Oh No!*, anchoring the band's front line across multiple horns.

A rotating cast of Chicago's top-shelf musicians from the jazz, jam, improvisational experimental music, and rock scenes regularly joins the band.
